# Banned phrases and patterns (Post Studio)

Treat this as a living list, not a fixed blocklist. LinkedIn clichés rotate — what's cringe today wasn't three years ago, and new ones emerge constantly. Whenever a judge (`post-studio.md` step 5) flags a phrase or pattern not listed here as fake/hype/cringe, add it to the list below before finishing the session, so the next post benefits from it.

## Banned phrases
- "game-changer"
- "here's what nobody tells you"
- "let that sink in"
- "I'm humbled"
- "thrilled to announce"
- "unlock"
- "in today's fast-paced world"
- "Agree?" (engagement bait)

## Banned patterns
- Fake humility ("I don't usually brag, but...")
- Fake epiphanies ("And then it hit me...")
- Filler rule-of-three lists (three vague items with no real content, just for rhythm)
- Rhetorical-question hooks used only to bait comments, not because the question is genuinely open
- Em dashes, period. Don't use them anywhere, not just "heavy" use — split into two sentences, use a comma, or use `|`
- "It's not X, it's Y" parallelism used more than once in a single post
- Excessive signposting ("Here's the thing:", "The truth is:", "Let's be honest:")

## How to extend this list
When a judge's one-line verdict names a specific phrase or pattern as the reason for a low `non_cringe` or `credibility` score, and it isn't already above, append it under the matching section with a one-line note on what made it land wrong. Keep entries short — a phrase or a one-sentence pattern description, not an essay.
