# Strategy and Calendar

## Positioning (one sentence)
"I help [specific client] get [specific result] with [specific thing I build]." Pressure-test it: could a stranger repeat it back? If not, narrow it.

## Content pillars (default mix, adjust to brief)
1. **Build-in-public** (35%): real projects, decisions, bugs, trade-offs, screenshots/demos.
2. **Trend takes** (25%): news viewed through hands-on experience (use Post Studio).
3. **Lessons/how-to** (20%): useful, specific, short; a client could learn something.
4. **Proof & offer** (20%): case studies, results, how you work, availability, the flagship offer; every one links to a real asset.
Never fill a pillar with a fabricated story; skip it that week instead.

## Cadence
Default 3 posts/week + 15 min/day of commenting (see pipeline.md). Scale to the user's stated time. Consistency over volume; say so.

## 30-day calendar
Table: date | pillar | topic/angle | format (text, text+image, carousel .pptx, demo video) | source of truth (which real project/news backs it). Mark which slots need Post Studio research. Leave 20% of slots flexible for news.

## Experiments
Pick 2 things to test per month (e.g. hook style, posting time, image vs text-only). Define what to measure beforehand and review in Weekly Review.
