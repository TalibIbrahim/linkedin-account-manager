# Offer (what you sell)

Clients buy outcomes, not "full-stack developer". Package, don't list skills.

## Build 2-3 productized offers
Each: **Name** | **Who it's for** | **Outcome** | **Scope (included / not included)** | **Timeline** | **Price (or starting at)** | **Process (3-5 steps)** | **Proof it works** (a real project).
Examples of shapes (adapt to the user's real skills, don't copy blindly): "MVP in 4 weeks for early-stage founders", "AI chatbot/RAG over your docs", "Performance and bug-fix sprint", "Next.js site/dashboard rebuild".
Pick one flagship offer; the rest are supporting.

## Pricing
Don't invent numbers. Ask target market and currency, then `web_search` current freelance rate ranges for that stack and market, show the range and sources, and help choose a defensible price based on scope and value to the client. Prefer fixed-scope or milestone pricing for early clients; advise a deposit. Frame as information to decide with, not financial advice.

## Cross-border freelancers (e.g. Pakistan-based, selling to US/UK/EU clients)
If the user is based somewhere where clients are mostly international (Pakistan is the common case here, but apply the same approach for any cross-border setup), don't hardcode payment or timezone advice — it goes stale and varies by corridor. Instead `web_search` for current:
- **Payment methods and fees**: options like Payoneer, Wise, direct bank wire, Deel/Remote-style EOR payouts, or platform escrow (Upwork/Fiverr) for the user's specific country↔client-country corridor; compare fees and settlement time, show sources.
- **Pricing currency and framing**: usually quote in USD/GBP/EUR to match client expectations, but confirm against current norms for the user's market and the client's region.
- **Timezone overlap**: find the actual working-hours overlap between the user's timezone and the client's (US/UK/EU), and suggest a realistic daily overlap window to offer for calls/async handoff — don't guess at overlap hours, compute them from the user's stated timezone.
- **Trust signals international clients look for**: payment protection norms, invoicing/contract expectations, time-zone-first language in the profile ("overlaps 4h/day with EST").
Treat all of this as current, sourced information to decide with, not fixed rules — rates, fees, and platform terms change.

## Where the offer appears
- Headline and first 3 lines of About
- LinkedIn Services section (set up categories, description, link) and Featured
- A clear CTA: email/booking link, "DM me 'MVP'", etc.
- Proof/offer posts (see strategy.md)
Output: offer sheet, one-liner, headline/About rewrites, Services section copy.
